# AI covenant

A model is a tool, and the person who submits a change answers for it.[^ai_covenant]

This repository renders someone else's vocabulary. So the covenant adds rules of its own: every element rests on a statement of the pinned Turtle, no rule goes beyond what DPV states, and a mapping is DPV's own or absent.[^ai_covenant]
